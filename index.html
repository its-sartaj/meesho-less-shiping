How to Test on Meesho Seller Panel

Empirical 4-step workflow to verify whether Meesho quotes lower shipping for your product listing.

Step 1

Upload Product Photo

Drop your raw product photo above. The client-side AI algorithm auto-detects subject boundaries.

Step 2

Generate 5 Studio Variations

Generates Standard White (82%), Soft Studio (80%), Tight Framing (88%), Generous Whitespace (74%), and Warm Studio (84%).

Step 3

Test Each on Meesho

Click "Download JPG" (or "Test on Meesho" via extension) to place the image into your draft product slot on Meesho Supplier Panel.

Step 4

Record Fee & Pick Winner

Record the actual shipping charge shown by Meesho (₹45, ₹50, ₹56, or ₹80). The dashboard highlights the biggest savings!

How to Install Extension in Chrome / Edge / Brave

  1. Download this repository as ZIP from GitHub and unzip it.
  2. Open Chrome and navigate to chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn ON Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked in the top-left toolbar.
  5. Select the unzipped folder containing manifest.json.
  6. Open supplier.meesho.com, create or edit a draft catalog, and use 1-click test & fee detection!
